# Audio credits

Every sound and all three music stems in `audio/` are original and synthesized from code by
`audio/tools/synth_audio.py` (numpy, scipy). No recordings or third-party samples are used.

License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), made for this project.

To replace a sound with a recording later, drop an `.ogg` with the same name into the same folder.
If it comes from a CC0 or CC-BY pack, list it here with its author, source URL and license.
